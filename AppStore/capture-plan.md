# Continuous screenshot set

Create one continuous **14,400 × 1,800 px** master and export five consecutive **2,880 × 1,800 px** tiles. Keep the master and native captures local under ignored `.local/app-store/` or `build/` until approved.

Use an off-white canvas, graphite type and one restrained colour accent carried through the five tiles. A thin continuous curve or quiet colour field can cross the tile boundaries. Leave generous space. Keep all words and important app controls clear of joins. Every tile must also work alone because the App Store adds gaps and may reflow its gallery.

Use actual captures from the final native app, with the built-in **Example** state and fictional data. Preserve native controls and proportions; crop or scale the captured interface, without inventing controls or replacing data in the screenshot. The sample label must remain legible. Do not use Elysium, Sidequest or Gair Rhydd production records, private endpoints or credentials.

| Tile | Exact heading | Native capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Your projects. At a glance. | Compact menu-bar panel, three sample projects. |
| 02 | Connect. See what matters. | Add Project flow with a safe example address and automatic KPI preview. |
| 03 | Choose your numbers. | KPI menu open with checked and unchecked choices. |
| 04 | A little more, when needed. | One expanded drawer showing extra KPIs and a short fictional breakdown. |
| 05 | Keep your numbers close. | Actual Project and Overview desktop widgets with sample numbers. |

No paragraphs or extra feature badges. Use a small Pulse wordmark once. Use the same baselines, scale and spacing across the set. Keep the app the visual focus; avoid device frames and repeated isolated screenshot cards.

Export flattened RGB/sRGB PNGs with no transparency. Names: `01-overview.png`, `02-connect.png`, `03-kpis.png`, `04-details.png`, `05-widgets.png`. Retain native originals and the continuous master. Review the full banner, each tile at full size, and reduced gallery size. Verify dimensions, order, text clipping, sample labels and absence of private data.

Apple’s current Mac specification accepts 16:10 images at 1280×800, 1440×900, 2560×1600 or 2880×1800. A screenshot set accepts 1–10 JPEG/PNG files. The screenshot gallery must show the app in use; Apple permits text overlays and asks for fictional account information rather than real-person data.

Sources checked 31 October 2026: [Screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications), [Upload screenshots](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots/), [Review guidelines 2.3.3 and 2.3.9](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata).
